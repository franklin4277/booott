START AND STOP THE TRADING PLATFORM
===================================

Open PowerShell and paste the command block you want to use.

START
-----
Set-Location 'C:\Users\frank\bottttt\booott'
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\start-all.ps1

Dashboard:
http://127.0.0.1:8000/dashboard

WARNING: Live trading is enabled in the current configuration.
If MT5 is connected to a real account and a signal passes the AI and risk checks,
the platform can submit a real order automatically. For testing, confirm MT5
is connected to a DEMO account before starting.

STOP
----
Set-Location 'C:\Users\frank\bottttt\booott'
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\stop-all.ps1

Stopping the services does not close or change positions already open in MT5.
Manage existing positions in the MT5 terminal.
